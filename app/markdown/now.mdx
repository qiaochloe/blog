## Employment

I have been gainfully employed this summer. Yes! 

I have been working as a SPOC (Systems Programmer, Operator, and Consultant) for my dear department's TStaff. My project is on creating a [common course container](https://cs.brown.edu/about/system/course-tech/common-course-containers/) that could support the needs of all the CS courses in the department.

## Future employment

I have found work in the CS Department to be meaningful. It has brought me a great deal of happiness and fulfillment. But I cannot escape the fact that... I am about to graduate. I need to find some other form of meaningful employment _soon_, or I really will go crazy out in the real world. So there's that.

I spent this summer brushing up [algorithms](/algorithms) and [data structures](/data-structures). Unfortunately, while I love learning things like [topological sort](/algorithms#topological-sort) and [union-find](/data-structures#union-find), I just can't bring myself to do LeetCode consistently. Maybe I'll be more locked-in in the fall.

## Gym arc

I have been going to the gym, [climbing](/climbing), and playing DDR this summer. 

It's kind of surprising how much progress you can make in a hobby when you spend a few hours on it each week. I didn't climb at all last semester, so I started the summer off on V2s and V3s. I just finished the last of the V3s in the gym late August, and now I'm working through the V4s. If you're around Providence and want to go climbing at some point, hit me up.

I don't have the numbers for DDR, but I recently broke through Expert 14. I feel like I was pretty stuck on Hard 8 and Hard 9 at the beginning of the summer, but the grading scheme in DDR is pretty song-pack-dependent.
