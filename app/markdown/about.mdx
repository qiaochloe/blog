## Who I Am

I am a student at Brown University studying computer science. I am particularly interested in networks, compilers, and solvers.

My [courses](/courses) and my work is a major part of my life. In the CS department, I have worked as a [systems programmer](https://cs.brown.edu/people/staff/spoc/), a [lab consultant](https://cs.brown.edu/degrees/undergrad/jobs/consult/), and a teaching assistant for [Formal Proof and Verification](https://browncs1951x.github.io/), [Computer Systems](https://csci0300.github.io/), and [Applied Cryptography](https://cs.brown.edu/courses/csci1515). I have also been the president of [Brown CS DUG](https://browncsdug.com/). Outside of the CS department, I help run the tech for [Brown Puzzlehunt](https://brownpuzzlehunt.com). I write about my working life here [[1]](/fall-2024) [[2]](/spring-2025) [[3]](/summer-2024).

## Principles

I want to conduct my life with [usefulness and reputation](https://www.brown.edu/about/mission).

When it comes down to it, the most important thing in life is **what you can do** and **how you treat people**. In that regard, who you become or who you are now is not as important as what you do.
